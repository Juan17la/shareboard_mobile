# Getting started

Shareboard needs no account and no sign-up. Open it and draw.

## 1. Open Shareboard

### In a browser (any computer, tablet or phone)

Go to **<https://shareboard-web.vercel.app>**. It works in any recent Chrome,
Edge, Firefox or Safari, on iPhone and iPad too.

### On Android

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Juan17la/shareboard_mobile/releases/latest)
   on your phone and download the `shareboard-v….apk` file.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks whether your browser or file manager
   may install apps: allow it once ("Install unknown apps").
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

The app is not on the Play Store yet, which is why Android asks.

**Updates:** when a new version is published, the app offers it the next time
you open it (**Download** / **Later**). **Download** fetches the new APK;
installing it over the old one keeps your boards and settings.

> There is no iPhone app. On an iPhone or iPad, use the web version: it does
> everything the app does.

## 2. Your first board

Shareboard always opens on **your own board**, kept on this device. The header
says *Only on this device*. It works without internet and is still there the
next time you open the app.

1. Pick the **pencil** in the toolbar and draw with your finger, mouse or pen.
2. Tap the colour box in the options strip to change the colour or width.
3. Pick a **shape** (rectangle, circle, triangle, polygon, line, arrow) and
   drag on the board to draw it.
4. Pick **T** and tap the board to write text.
5. Pick the **cursor** to select, move, resize and rotate what you drew.

Two fingers move the board and pinch to zoom. On a computer, use the mouse
wheel to move and **Ctrl + wheel** to zoom.

A short tutorial walks new users through these steps the first time. You can
replay it from **Settings → Replay the tutorial**.

## 3. Draw together

1. Tap **Share**. Because your first board lives only on your device,
   Shareboard asks to **create a board to share**: it uploads a copy and opens
   it. Your private board stays as it was.
2. The share sheet shows the board's **six-character code** (for example
   `K7M·Q2R`), a **link**, and a **QR code**. On Android you can send the link
   straight to WhatsApp or any other app.
3. Others open the link, scan the QR code, or type the code in
   **+ New / Join → Join with a code**.

Everyone sees each other's drawing as it happens, plus a coloured cursor with
each person's name.

## 4. Choose your name and icon

Until you choose one, you appear as *Guest 1234*. Open **Settings** to set your
nickname and an icon (🦊 🐼 🐸 …); the icon's colour becomes your cursor
colour. Settings is also where you switch between **Spanish and English** and
between the **light and dark board**.

## 5. Find your boards again

**⋯ menu → My whiteboards** lists the boards you opened
recently, with your device's own board first. Boards are kept on the server, so
any board you have the code for can be opened again from any device.
